Rules: write what's rapped, and put ad-libs and non-lead vocals in (parentheses)
  • Write what is actually rapped, not what was published.
  • Delete an opening or closing line that falls outside the clip. The draft pads 3 s before the window.
  • Put anything that isn't the lead rapper's main line in (parentheses): the lead's own ad-libs ("yeah", "uh", doubled words), a sung hook, a chorus, a background singer. Still write the words as heard.
  • Parenthesised words are optional in scoring. A model is scored against the text with them and without them, and keeps the better result.
  • If the published lyrics don't match the audio at all, leave the clip as a draft and say so in the note.
  • Keep one bar per line. Punctuation and capitals don't affect the score.
